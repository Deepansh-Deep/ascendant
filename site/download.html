Install on Android

  1. Tap the download button and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to "install unknown apps". This appears because Ascendant is installed from this website, not the Play Store.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, choose "Install anyway".
  4. Open Ascendant and create your account, or sign in.
Updating: the app tells you when a new version is out. Download it from here and install it over the old one. Your training stays, because it is saved to your account.

iPhone or computer

Open the web app in Safari or Chrome. On iPhone, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
