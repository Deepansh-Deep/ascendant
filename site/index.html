Your aura shows how hard you train

Pick your hunter. Every level, every rank, every best you beat and every day of your streak makes the aura around them stronger. On days you train, it burns brightest. Each rank brings a new colour, from a faint spirit at rank E to a crimson shadow at rank S.

Nothing to buy. Every aura, badge and title is earned in the gym.

A real tracker first

Logging a set takes two taps. Everything works without internet and syncs when you are back online.

  • Sets, reps, weight and rest timerYour weekly split, your exercises, last session and best set shown on every exercise.
  • Personal records, liveThe moment a set beats your best, you see it, with the previous record beside it.
  • Progress you can readWeekly volume, consistency, strength trend per lift, muscle balance, bodyweight.
  • XP, ranks, challenges, badgesDaily and weekly challenges checked against your logs. Missed days stay missed.

Your gym becomes your guild

Members join their gym's guild with a code, and the gym approves each request. Leaderboards run weekly, monthly and all time.

  • Four ways to winConsistency, Strength, Progress and Overall. Not just who has trained longest.
  • Fair to beginnersConsistency and Progress compare you with your own past. Strength is relative to your bodyweight.
  • Scores nobody can type inThe server recalculates every score from synced workouts with published formulas. Purchases never change a rank.
