Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains what Ascendant collects and why. We collect as little as the app needs to work.

What we store

  • Account: username, display name, and your password, stored only as a secure hash by our database provider (Supabase).
  • Training: the workouts, sets, check-ins, plan, bodyweight entries and hunter choice you enter, so they sync between your devices and survive a lost phone.
  • Guild: which gym's guild you belong to and the four guild scores calculated from your training.
  • Gym profile: for guild masters, the gym's name, city and logo.

What stays on your phone

Progress photos never leave your phone. If you use the app without an account, all of your data stays on your phone.

Who can see what

  • Members of your guild see your username, display name and guild scores.
  • Your guild master sees the same, plus when you were last active.
  • The app owner can see account details to provide support, for example to reset a password.
  • We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Service providers

Supabase hosts the database and login. GitHub hosts this website and the app download.

Your choices

  • Leave a guild at any time from the Guild tab.
  • Delete all your training data from Settings, using "Start fresh". This removes it from the phone and the server.
  • To delete your account completely, contact us and we will do it within 30 days. Payment records may be kept where the law requires.

Children

Ascendant is not meant for children under 13.
