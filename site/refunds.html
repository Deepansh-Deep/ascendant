Refunds and delivery

Last updated 3 November 2026

Members

Ascendant is free for members. There are no in-app purchases, so there is nothing to refund.

Gym subscriptions

  • The first month is free. After that, subscriptions are billed monthly in advance.
  • You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops the next billing; the current month is not refunded in part.
  • If we cannot provide the service for a significant part of a paid month because of a fault on our side, we will extend your subscription or refund that month.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days of approval.
